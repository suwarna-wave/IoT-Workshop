Start with a clean IoT foundation and build forward.

This workshop introduces the core ideas behind IoT, then moves step by step into Arduino, ESP boards, communication protocols, sensors, and a final working prototype. The structure is intentionally simple so students can focus on learning, not navigation.

Sensor data

Direct ESP communication, mobile links, and fast local alerts.

RFID and modules

 Day page 7

Final prototype

Dashboard, integration, testing, and polished presentation flow.
